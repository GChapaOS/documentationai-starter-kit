## Overview

Schedule pickups to streamline your shipping flow.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Pickups">
    In your dashboard, click **Pickups** in the main menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click 'New Pickup'">
    Fill in details like address, date, and cargo type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm and submit">
    Click **Schedule Pickup** to confirm. You'll receive an email with details.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">
Pickup requests can be reviewed in the Pickups section.
</Callout>
