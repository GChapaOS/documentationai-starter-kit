---

## Problem

- Error: "Unable to create shipment"
- Create button is disabled

## Quick Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check required fields">
    Ensure all mandatory fields (destination, cargo, date) are filled in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refresh your browser">
    Sometimes a fresh session resolves form issues.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear browser cache">
    Try clearing cache and login again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
Contact soporte@cfcops.com if issue persists.
</Callout>
