## Overview

Track shipments easily from your dashboard.

## Steps

1. Log in at `https://dashboard.cfcops.com`
2. Navigate to **Shipments**
3. Enter the Shipment ID in the search bar
4. Click on the shipment to see real-time status and location

<Callout kind="info">
Shipment status updates automatically.
</Callout>
