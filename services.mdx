## Overview

CFC Ops gives you a simple way to match the right shipping service to the shipment you need to move. You can compare parcel, consolidated freight, import and export shipping, and full truckload options without having to guess which one fits best.

## How to choose the right service

Choose the service by looking at shipment size, transit urgency, handling requirements, and destination type. If you choose too small a service level, you may delay delivery or increase touchpoints. If you choose too large a service level, you may pay for unused capacity.

### Selection criteria

| Criterion | Best fit | Why it matters |
|----------|----------|----------------|
| Small parcel count | Parcel shipping | Keeps handling simple and cost-effective |
| Mixed freight with flexible timing | LTL consolidated freight | Shares cost across multiple shipments |
| Cross-border movement | Import and export shipping | Supports customs and handoff steps |
| High volume or dedicated load | Full truckload | Reduces transfers and improves control |

## Service details

### Parcel shipping

Parcel shipping is best for smaller shipments that move as separate units. It works well when you need predictable handling, frequent dispatches, and straightforward tracking.

<CodeGroup tabs="JavaScript,cURL">
  ```javascript
const shipment = {
  service: "parcel",
  pieces: 6,
  totalWeightKg: 48,
  origin: "Monterrey, NL",
  destination: "Querétaro, QRO",
  serviceLevel: "standard"
};

console.log("Create parcel shipment:", shipment);
  ```
  ```bash
curl -X POST "https://api.example.com/shipments" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_TOKEN" \
  -d '{
    "service": "parcel",
    "pieces": 6,
    "totalWeightKg": 48,
    "origin": "Monterrey, NL",
    "destination": "Querétaro, QRO",
    "serviceLevel": "standard"
  }'
  ```
</CodeGroup>

### LTL consolidated freight

LTL means less-than-truckload. You use it when your freight does not require a full trailer and you can combine it with other shipments. This option is useful for pallets, mixed cartons, and recurring regional delivery patterns.

### Import and export shipping

Import and export shipping helps you plan the steps between countries or customs zones. You usually use it when documents, timing, and handoff control matter as much as transport capacity.

### Full truckload options

Full truckload is the right choice when you need exclusive trailer space, fewer stops, or tighter control over the route. It is often used for high-volume freight, time-sensitive deliveries, or cargo that should not be combined with other loads.

## Planning checklist

Before you confirm a shipment, verify the following:

- The shipment size matches the selected service.
- The destination supports the required delivery schedule.
- Handling needs are clear for fragile or high-value freight.
- Required paperwork is complete.
- The quoted service level matches the customer expectation.

<Expandable title="Advanced service selection tips" default-open="false">

If you manage recurring shipments, group them by lane, weight range, and delivery urgency. This helps you standardize service selection and reduce manual review.

You can also define routing rules for common scenarios, such as parcel for under a certain threshold, LTL for palletized freight, and full truckload for dedicated moves.
