## Welcome to CFC Ops

CFC Ops helps you organize project documentation in one place so you can keep your team aligned and your workflows easy to find. This starter kit gives you a clean, flexible foundation that you can adapt to your own process, roles, and terminology.

Use this space to describe your core operations, highlight the most common tasks, and point people toward the pages they need first. If you manage shipping, customer access, service details, or internal references, this documentation can help you present those topics in a clear and consistent way.

## Account and access overview

Your documentation should make it obvious who can use the system, what they can see, and where they should go when they need support. Keep access rules simple and practical. If a user needs a different role, a reset, or a new permission, document that path clearly so they can follow it without extra help.

You can also use this page to set expectations for internal teams and external users. For example, note which tasks are public, which ones require an account, and which ones should be handled by a support contact or an operations lead.
